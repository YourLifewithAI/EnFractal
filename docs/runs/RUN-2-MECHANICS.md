# Run 2: the baseline mechanics (planned 14 October)

**Read with** [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [RUN-2-STATUS.md](RUN-2-STATUS.md), [ORCHESTRATION.md](ORCHESTRATION.md), [OWNERSHIP.md](OWNERSHIP.md), the design this round builds, [docs/game/MECHANICS.md](../game/MECHANICS.md), and the designs it leans on: [the magic design](../magic/README.md), [the traits draft](../world/TRAITS.md), [the characters](RUN-2-CHARACTERS.md), [the torch round](RUN-2-TORCH.md), [contracts/README.md](../../contracts/README.md). **Status: planned 14 October 2026 from the founder's edited design, squared against the code by eight readers. Wave 0 (M0, the contract round; its seams under section 3) and all of Wave 1 (M1 to M6) were built and merged overnight on 14 to 15 October ([RUN-2-STATUS.md](RUN-2-STATUS.md), the fourteenth session). Next: the Wave 1 review, a small contract round (the Fractal spend at a camp), then Wave 2.**

This page is the build plan. It turns the design into packets that agents build side by side without touching each other's files, says which model takes each one and on which machine, and names what the founder judges at the end of each wave.

## 1. What this round builds

A **team** (one person and their Gubble) as one folder on disk; three meters (hearts in quarters, a stamina bar, pips round the Gubble); **connection energy** that charges the Gubble's abilities by who directed them; growth counted from **Fractals** (bands of stamina, hearts) and **expertise**; **knacks** chosen at creation; an **opening menu**; Challenge **difficulties**; **Trust** from receipts; the **Bubble** that carries the player; a **minimap**; and the **clock** record that Live will need. The design holds every number as provisional data.

**The first playable slice** is two packets, M1 and M2: the energy pool, its prices and the three meters, played for one evening on the garage island in Challenge. It ships at the end of Wave 1's first two merges, before Wave 2 starts.

## 2. How the work is split

- **No two packets in a wave touch the same file.** Ownership follows [OWNERSHIP.md](OWNERSHIP.md); the grants this round adds are listed in section 7. A packet that needs a change elsewhere writes the diff in its report.
- **Routing** follows [ORCHESTRATION.md](ORCHESTRATION.md): **Opus** for the kernel, contracts, persistence, the companion boundary and every review; **Sonnet** for the HUD, the body's feel, the generator and the look. Each packet names its model.
- **Machines.** DiamondAge takes at most two builders at a time and keeps the founder's evenings free. Packets marked *headless* run as Linux cloud sessions (`tools/linux/setup-toolchain.sh`, then `tools/linux/test-all.sh`); packets marked *GPU* need DiamondAge for captures or feel. So a wave of six runs as four in the cloud and two here.
- **Every packet:** a worktree `C:\dev\EnFractal-run2\<packet>` (junctions `.cache\dotnet` and `.cache\godot` into the integrator's cache) or a cloud session; a branch `run2/<packet>`; lean testing (targeted while iterating, the full runners once at the end); mutation checks at most five; failing-before evidence for anything called a bug; a report of about 250 words with the final pass and fail lines, decisions for the founder and commit hashes.
- **Seams are contracts.** Wave 0 fixes every shape two packets share (a schema field, an op, a reason, a store's path, the vitals' ledger calls), so a packet builds against the contract and a stub until its neighbour merges. The list is in M0.
- **Reviews.** One fresh Opus reviewer after Wave 1 (the kernel, the saves and the body), one after Wave 2 (the menu, Trust and the Bubble). The integrator reviews each merge: scope, test lines, one spot check.

## 3. Wave 0: the contract round (M0)

**The integrator, Opus, headless, alone; everything after builds against it.**

- **The rules pack** (`contracts/island-rules.schema.json`), new optional blocks, each defaulting to today's behaviour when absent:
  - `traits`: hearts (health per quarter 5, start 5 hearts, cap 30; one quarter healed every 2.5 s after 10 s without harm on safe ground), stamina (start 0.4 m, band 0.4 m, ten bands, the running cost per second, the refill pace, the deep-water and dive drains, the half-speed rule at empty), pips (start 8, one per five expertise, ceiling 40, refill near and far, the camp's half pool), the fall cap and the fire rate (one quarter a second), the wallet's start.

## 11. Terms

*Packet:* one unit of work for one agent on files it owns. *Wave:* packets that run side by side because they share no file. *Seam:* a shape two packets both depend on, fixed in Wave 0. *Principal:* the identity the game stamps on a command from the connection it arrived on. *Receipt:* the game's own note of what a command did. *Consequence fact:* the journal's record that something the Gubble did hurt the player or their builds. *Headless:* no window, as the test runners run. *Grant:* permission for one packet to edit named lines of a file it does not own, for this round only.
