# Phase 0 product and feasibility decision record

**Status:** working record for E01, 1 October 2026. It distinguishes the founder's stated direction from engineering proposals. It does not mark the phase 0 gate passed. Read with the [roadmap](../../roadmap/ROADMAP.md), [backlog](../../roadmap/BACKLOG.md), [world vision](../../WORLD-VISION.md), and [first engine slice](../first-slice.md).

| Question | Current decision or evidence | Status before phase 0 exit |
|---|---|---|
| Why build it? | A geographically recognizable, shared Earth where people explore and make permissioned, persistent, structurally editable inventions; personal sandbox Earths permit bounded rule changes and invitations. The first proof is a place and creative loop worth returning to, not a detailed planet. | Founder direction confirmed in the world vision and prior conversation. |
| Art | Grounded painterly 3D: natural scale, soft sculpted forms, selective detail, coherent tactile materials and gentle lighting. Recognizable modern buildings remain modern. Style must reapply to later edits and have a low-detail treatment. | Founder direction confirmed; exact asset grammar, shaders and quality bar remain to be demonstrated. |
| First geography | Barton Creek Greenbelt near the mall and MoPac/360, centered on **30.250924, -97.810494**. The current 4,096 m package is an engineering fixture; the roadmap proposes a smaller playable core. | Location confirmed; playable boundary and permissions are not chosen. Source height datum and several illustrative features remain uncertain. |
| First 15 minutes | Proposed solo path: choose a creature, explore a recognizable ridge/garden route, walk/glide, operate a bounded wind device, change one approved part, preview it, save an exhibit and find it on return. A cooperative consent interaction follows when a second player joins. Core activity must work with AI off. | Product hypothesis from roadmap; founder walkthrough and observed playtest are still needed. Current prototype supports walking/gliding plus a local platform and connected path, but not the wind device, creature choice or return-use loop. |
| AI role | Phase 5 plans optional AI-assisted creation using the same compiler and permissions as manual editing. An embodied companion/avatar is currently a later expansion. | **Founder priority unresolved.** The founder has described wanting to build and play *with* an AI agent; decide whether a narrowly scoped visible companion belongs in the private MVP or remains later. Do not count the present local workshop as this experience. |
| Development capacity and spending | One founder plus coding agents; early monthly hosting and AI spend under **$100**. Paying-player revenue goes back into development after operating obligations and reserve. Delay paid hosting until remote testing needs it. | Founder confirmed. The roadmap's $95 envelope is a proposed operating allocation, not measured cost. |
| Client and host | Native Windows client and Linux headless host are the current candidates. Godot is installed and runs the local Barton Creek fixture. Godot + C# is a hypothesis; the current fixture uses GDScript. | E03 must prove export/headless viability, maintained encrypted/authenticated transport path and measured resource use before production commitment. Host location/provider has not been selected; measure cohort latency and cost first. |
| Camera/avatar | A third-person creature with adjustable camera and modest initial sizes is the roadmap assumption. The fixture's first-person controller is temporary. | Founder review of camera, embodiment, creature sizes and comfort settings remains open. Do not infer support for ant-scale or continent-scale play. |
| Test hardware | The development laptop has i7-10875H, about 32 GiB RAM, RTX 2070 Super and Intel integrated graphics. The roadmap also targets a real 8 GiB integrated-graphics device. | Installed hardware is observed, but neither the laptop nor simulated low memory certifies the minimum target. E03/E06/E08 need captured timings and a real low-device run. |
| Cohort and distribution | Invited adult research testers, initially ten and then up to twenty activated people, are planning assumptions. No public registration or paid service is required in phases 0–7. | Founder must select audience/age policy and conduct/privacy process before enrolling people. A public or paid launch needs the roadmap's legal and operational review. |

**Phase 0 stop risks:** inability to produce a supported native/headless build; a licensed, reproducible and geographically honest map fixture; a maintained authenticated/encrypted transport path; or a measurable route to acceptable performance on the intended device. A failed gate triggers a scoped decision record and narrower alternative, not a claim that phase 0 passed. The [proposed contract](contract-v0.1.md) records the interfaces that E03–E08 can test while unresolved product choices stay visible.
