# Props drawn by the family

Things characters carry, made by the same drawing-to-character converter (`pipeline/characters/conv_a`), in its format: `character.glb` (metres, +Y up, facing -Z, pivot at the bottom centre, 0.10 m tall as the converter scales everything; the game sets each prop's own size), `character.json`, `interpretation.json` and the shared turntable's renders.

- `torch/`: the Gubble's torch, taken out of the Gubble's round-2 interpretation unchanged on 13 October 2026, when the founder made it a separate item: made by the Gubble's Glow (the Torch discovery), carried by the Gubble or the player, always lit until put out, and able to start fires. The handle is the root part and the flame (`torch_flame`, a `flicker` effect) its child. Not yet in the game: see the torch's plan in `docs/runs/`.
