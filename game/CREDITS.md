# EnFractal art credits

## Original painterly reference assets

The editable live oak and Ashe juniper sources (`assets/art_sources/painterly/trees/*.blend`), their exported meshes, the painted textures (bark, ground, limestone, grass tuft, leaf clusters) and the painterly shaders are original EnFractal work from the earlier outdoor art pass. They are retained as reference material and as a starting point for outdoor test rooms and for creations. They are not captured from any real place and claim no surveyed accuracy.

## Captured rooms

Rooms produced by the capture pipeline are built from the player's own photographs. Generated object assets are derived from those photographs by the pipeline's reconstruction and image-to-3D backends; the backends in use and their licenses are recorded per room in that room's manifest.

## Historical

Earlier builds used USGS 3DEP elevation and OpenStreetMap data for Austin-area maps. That data and its notices were removed with the geography layer; see the `geography-era-final` tag for the historical packages and their `COPYING.md` files.
